# Idle Dyson Swarm advertising campaign

Research date: 4 September 2026, Australia/Sydney. Repository inspected at `21df265d`.

Status: proposed campaign and production brief. Budgets are illustrative Australian dollars, not authorised spending or platform price quotes. No advertising, outreach, store changes, or tracking implementation has been launched.

## Recommendation

Position Idle Dyson Swarm as a deep space incremental built around automation, build choices, and controlled use of offline time. Lead with **“Build a swarm. Break Infinity.”**

Start with existing players, r/incremental_games, genre creators, and an updated incrementalDB listing. Test paid Reddit community audiences and Apple Ads search results with a small, capped budget. Treat mobile as the first paid acquisition destination; use Steam primarily for organic discovery and reactivation until its conversion and current player experience are understood.

The first month should establish which message brings players who stay. There is no evidence here establishing profitable acquisition costs. Current revenue, retention, payer conversion, country mix, and advertising account history were not available in the inspected material. Public downloads and reviews show reach and sentiment, not revenue or current active players.

Fans of Swarm Simulator, Antimatter Dimensions, and Unnamed Space Idle are useful audience hypotheses, not proven cross-purchase segments. Space/factory fans are secondary: showing a beautiful 3D fleet would attract expectations the actual game does not fulfil. The ad should visibly be a numbers-and-systems incremental from its first seconds.

Core description: “Build bots, automate a solar empire, and push through Infinity. Shape your progress with research, skill choices, and Stored Time in Idle Dyson Swarm.”

Use one primary benefit per asset. Automation is the accessible hook; builds demonstrate depth; Stored Time is the differentiator. Keep late progression and story as a glimpse rather than a list of unexplained proper nouns.

## Where to advertise

### 1. Existing community and r/incremental_games

Publish a useful current-version walkthrough to the existing community, followed by one substantive developer post with playable links, a short gameplay clip, and a clear explanation of what changed. Identify yourself as the developer. Invite specific feedback about the first session and Stored Time, and answer it promptly.

The live subreddit rules URL did not expose usable rule text in this research session. Check its current posting rules and required disclosures before publication; no historical posting frequency is assumed. Avoid repeated promotional posts or unsolicited private messages.

For paid Reddit, start with the `incremental_games` community audience if selectable in Ads Manager. Community targeting reaches people who engaged with selected communities across Reddit; it does not reserve placement inside that subreddit. Available targets must be confirmed in the account. Disable automatic expansion where the chosen campaign permits it during the initial test. [Reddit targeting documentation](https://www.business.reddit.com/advertise/targeting/community-and-interest)

Use Android traffic to Google Play for the initial paid test. Test two ads in the same country and audience so the primary difference is the hook. Later, test iOS or a separate automation audience such as `Dyson_Sphere_Program` if available. Do not mix these with the first comparison.

### 2. Incremental-game creators

Prioritise a readable first-look or beginner-guide video over a generic sponsorship read. This game's decisions benefit from explanation.

Two concrete leads:

[IDS video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z5AzPQtKa58), [historical channel/video index](https://www.letsplayindex.com/channels/2842015-kosh-idle/videos/page-5).

Before contacting either, check their latest ten comparable uploads, median views, audience platform, and current business-contact preference. Send a personalised editorial invitation first; the game is free, so a key has little value. Offer a concise guide, clean footage, and optional legitimate progression examples. Never require praise. ### 3. Apple Ads search results

Use search results to reach people already looking for an incremental game. Start with exact-match category terms: `incremental game`, `idle space game`, `space idle`, and `idle strategy`. These are candidate keywords, not verified search-volume opportunities. Keep Search Match off for the controlled category test; add discovery later with a separate budget. Apple's documented structure separates brand, category, competitor, and discovery themes. [Campaign structure](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/help/campaigns/0056-structure-campaigns), [match types](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/help/keywords/0059-understand-keyword-match-types).

Use one English-speaking country first. Choose the strongest existing country by retained users and net revenue from the store consoles; if those data are unavailable, Australia is a manageable default for an operational pilot, not evidence of highest returns. Low search volume is inconclusive, not proof the creative failed. Expand countries sequentially instead of splitting a tiny budget across eight languages.

Do not make brand search the main acquisition test: existing demand can make it look artificially efficient. Later, create a custom product page whose first screenshots match the winning automation or Stored Time angle. [Apple ad variations](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/best-practices/ad-variations).

### 4. incrementalDB and Steam organic discovery

Update the existing incrementalDB entry with current platform links, accurate visual tags, and genuine gameplay screenshots. Treat it as editorial discovery; paid placement availability was not established.

For Steam, prepare a current-version guide and an announcement about verifiable player-facing changes. Check whether an Update Visibility Round is available for a qualifying update. Valve provides five initial rounds, each running up to 30 days or one million impressions; seasonal sales can interrupt display while the clock continues. Remaining entitlement and eligibility are account-specific and unverified. [Valve documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/visibility/update_rounds).

### Channels to defer

- **Broad Meta, TikTok, and display buying:** keep the first budget concentrated. Test short gameplay clips organically before financing distribution. Their priority here is a campaign judgment, not evidence those networks cannot work.
- **Google App campaigns:** revisit once acquisition value and a larger budget are established. Google recommends daily budget of at least 50 times target CPI for its target-CPI install strategy; a hypothetical A$2 target implies A$100/day. This is guidance for that strategy, not a universal minimum for every Google campaign. [Google guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7678575?hl=en).
- **Broad science/space audiences and large generalist influencers:** the thematic overlap is weaker than demonstrated interest in incrementals. Require a small comparative test before expanding.
- **Portfolio cross-promotion boosts:** the repo records these as deferred product ideas, so they are not campaign inventory. Ordinary promotion through existing owned channels can be planned separately. [Deferred direction](future-monetization-and-progression.md).

Proposed working masters: 1080×1920 vertical, 1080×1080 square, and 1920×1080 landscape. Check each destination's current export requirements before upload. First three screenshot concepts: **Build your production engine**, **Shape your next run**, **Put Stored Time to work**. Follow with a later-progression glimpse. Avoid personal saves, debug-only results, unreleased controls, unsupported cloud-save claims, or cinematic art masquerading as gameplay. Use the soundtrack only after confirming advertising rights.

## Claims to use carefully

- “Free to play” matches the public store listings. Mobile listings show in-app purchases and the current catalog contains tips, Developer Options, and Double Infinity Points. Do not repeat historical “no IAP” claims or call all purchases cosmetic.
- Describe offline time as banked and processed under player control. Do not promise instant, perfectly equivalent offline simulation or an unlimited starting bank.
- Eight languages are implemented in this checkout and described on mobile listings; Steam's retrieved language table lists English only. Confirm shipped support on each advertised platform before localising ads.
- The repo is not proof a particular Steam/mobile build is already released. A returning-player ad must name changes verified on its destination.
- Do not advertise deferred cross-promotion boosts or galvanized skills. Avoid completion-time, infinite-content, perfect-save-recovery, and automatic cross-platform-sync promises.

## Measurement and stop rules

Keep a ledger by date, country, platform, source, campaign, and creative: spend, impressions, clicks, store visits, new downloads/activations, available retention, net proceeds, and qualitative issues. Separate new users from returning users. Never count a Steam activation as an engaged player or combine Apple, Google, and Steam metrics as though their definitions match.

- **Steam:** UTM links report trusted and logged-in tracked visits, plus free-to-play activations within a 72-hour conversion window. Activations are licences, not first sessions; in-game purchases are not UTM conversions. [Steam UTM documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/utm_analytics).
- **Apple:** generate links in App Store Connect, retaining the provider token and a unique campaign token. Reporting has delay and privacy thresholds; sparse results are not necessarily zero results. Use available campaign usage/retention reporting with its consent and coverage limitations. [Apple campaign links](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/acquisition/campaign-links/).
- **Google Play:** use source/campaign tags and console acquisition reports, verifying what dimensions are actually available. Reporting groups small cohorts and attribution can be incomplete. Do not assume campaign-level revenue or retention is exposed just because acquisition is. [Google Play reporting](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859173?hl=en).
- **Gameplay events:** a targeted repo search found development telemetry but no established campaign analytics pipeline. “First purchase”, “first Infinity”, and exact source-level D7 activity are proposed measurements, not existing capabilities. Prefer platform reporting initially; if it cannot support a conclusion, report the gap. Additional instrumentation is separate work and must match the game's privacy disclosures.

Example Steam campaign link:

`https://store.steampowered.com/app/4348570/Idle_Dyson_Swarm/?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=ids_202609_swarm&utm_content=automation_a`

Use the corresponding source/creative names for mobile links using each platform's supported generator or parameters. Preview links from the target device before spending. Keep organic, editorial creator, sponsored creator, and paid-social sources separate.

Primary decision: cost per retained player where attributable; otherwise report cost per new install alongside the platform-level retention trend without claiming a causal link. Click-through rate diagnoses the hook; store conversion diagnoses the promise/destination match. Neither establishes profitability.

Revenue rule: use net contribution per acquired player after platform deductions, refunds, and variable costs, over a declared payback window. An illustrative A$12 net revenue per payer at 2% payer conversion produces only A$0.24 per install before other costs. Those numbers are an example, not IDS data. If acquisition is for audience growth rather than direct payback, explicitly treat its ceiling as a marketing expense.

Operational rules, proposed rather than industry benchmarks:

1. Pause immediately for a broken link, wrong build, incorrect claim, or serious new-user/save issue reported on the promoted build.
2. Before launch choose a maximum acceptable cost per install based on measured contribution or an explicit learning allowance. Pause and investigate a variant after spending three times that amount with zero attributed installs, once the platform's reporting window has elapsed. This is a spending safeguard, not a statistical verdict.
3. Do not crown an ad from a few clicks or downloads. Seek at least 50 new installs per variant and matured D7 evidence for a directional comparison where the budget permits. This is not a statistical-power calculation. If the cap arrives first, classify the result as inconclusive and stop.
4. If clicks are plentiful but store conversion is weak, fix message/asset consistency before bidding more. If downloads convert but retention is poor, inspect onboarding and expectation mismatch.
5. Release reserve only after the winning cohort's quality is credible and its cost fits the chosen ceiling. Broad scale needs repeatable results and observed economics, not this pilot alone.
