## Economy and ownership

The explicit **Begin Farming Villages** action becomes available after the six opening Forager gates. Exactly three founders move once. Existing paid Forager receipts finish with the remaining camp's crew; no new Forager cycles begin. All old stocks and remaining people stay with that camp. Excess equipped kits that cannot remain with its workers are recorded with the transferred founders; they give no invented village bonus.

The retained camp becomes one no-input Gathering generator. Its productive labor is retained workers plus half-labor equipped workers. Original Gathering produces 1 Food and 3 Materials per 20 worker-seconds. Convert 100 old Food into 1 village Food and 300 old Materials into 1 village Material, capped at 2.7 village units/minute each. This unit boundary avoids copying the camp inventory or letting an old, enormous camp skip the age. A normal Balanced opening at 1180 seconds settles to 56 retained workers with 13 equipped workers: 62.5 labor, yielding **1.875 Food and 1.875 Materials/minute**. Exports respect village Food reservations/capacity and useful Materials demand. Actual fractured skills add their existing 5% speed once; unspent Catalysts add none.

Farming owns four stocks: Food, Materials, Tools and Goods. It begins with three founders, no duplicate starter inventory, and 80 Food storage. Each supported Home adds two residents. Each manually purchased Granary adds 160 Food storage; its nth price is ceil(35×2ⁿ) Materials and ceil(4×1.75ⁿ) Tools. Purchase is instant, never automatic, and protects the reserve for pending repairs.

The approved candidate's work values are converted from worker-minutes to worker-seconds, including its 0.7 construction/work factor. Fields produce 12 Food for 42 worker-seconds; Woodlot 8 Materials for 42; Workshop converts 6 Materials into 2 Tools for 42; established Kiln converts 4 Materials into 2 Goods for 63. Pasture multiplies Fields output by 1.5, Waterworks by another 1.25. The final Food batch can be partial. Its output remains reserved so exports cannot consume its capacity, and paid outputs are never discarded.

Home bills (Food / Materials / Tools / worker-seconds): 16/16/2/126, 100/90/6/420, 180/150/8/588, 280/220/10/756, 400/300/12/924, 550/400/14/1092. Pasture costs 120 Food, 120 Materials, 8 Tools and 672 work; Kiln 140 Materials, 10 Tools and 840; Waterworks 260 Materials, 18 Tools, 40 Goods and 1344; Hall 400 Materials, 24 Tools, 90 Goods and 2016. Hall shipments cost 350 Food and 35 Goods, require 1890 work, and return 80 Materials plus one trade connection. Six supported Homes and six connections complete the age.

Balanced / Supplies / Growth / Travel use the supplied weighted scheduler and upkeep fractions (15%, 4%, 20%, 2%). Travel uses Balanced labor before Hall. Paid work survives mode changes without another debit. One construction or shipment goal runs alongside useful production. Plans prioritize repairs, required storage and the candidate's Home/facility/connection gates.

Weathering changes per simulation minute: Balanced −1, Supplies +2, Growth −4, Travel +4. Waterworks cuts increases by 40%. At 75, the UI warns of housing risk; at 100, the most recently supported Home loses support and Weathering resets to 40. Founders remain; repair pays the original Materials/Tools/work bill, without repaying Food or adding a new lifetime Home.

Six one-time milestones use the existing Catalyst wallet: first Home, Pasture or Kiln, Waterworks, Hall, three connections, and age completion. **Farming-specific fracture nodes are not authored.** Eligibility is exactly the existing Skill tree transaction's authored unlock and non-fractured checks. If no eligible choice or wallet room exists, the award remains pending and visible. This is a design limit, not a claim that six Farming-specific choices exist.

## Save and Stored Time boundaries

Existing version-4 Forager saves remain unchanged. Only explicit entry creates Civilization version 5 with nested Farming version 1; older v2/v3 migration remains supported. Stocks, supported/lost Homes, lifetime bills, paid receipts, partial output, mode, clock remainder, founder ownership and awards round-trip through the normal validated serializer. Unknown funded recipes and duplicate owners are rejected.

Both worker-backed Stored Time and the active speed boost preview this owned economy before funding accelerated time. They stop at an affordable required manual Granary (or final age action), leave the remaining bank, and resume only after the player acts. The worker receives a smaller complete atomic request; its existing persistence, cancellation and exact bank-debit rules remain intact. Normal unboosted time can pass while the player reviews a manual gate.

The final **Establish the village** action stops new work and Weathering, finishes paid village cycles, and leaves one completed village view. First Cities and later-age consumers are not fabricated.
