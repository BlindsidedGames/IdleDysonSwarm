# Version 4.1.8

*In development — these notes will grow as work on the update continues.*

## Infinity Challenges and Galvanized Skills

- Added **Blank Slate**, the first Infinity Challenge, available after your first Infinity. Complete an Infinity without assigning ordinary skills to earn a Galvanizer. Galvanized skills remain active during the challenge. The reward is earned once; the challenge can be replayed.
- Spend a **Galvanizer** to make a revealed base skill permanently active, without using Skill Points. Any Skill Points already invested in that skill are returned. Galvanization is permanent and survives Infinity, Quantum, and Overflow resets.
- Galvanized skills lose their penalties and exclusions while keeping their normal activation conditions and timer resets. Galvanizing a skill does not reveal locked branches.
- Added three **Augments** to Galvanized Cash & Science: +5 seconds of Panel Lifetime, each decayed panel counts as 10, and double Cash and Science production. Each costs 1 Skill Point, all three can be assigned together, and they can be refunded and reset normally.
- Galvanized skills have a fractured appearance, with an Augment counter where available. The subtle fracture animation respects reduced-motion settings.

## Overflow

- Reaching **4e242 Bots** now lets you choose when to Overflow from Avocato, instead of automatically granting the old boundary reward.
- Overflow starts your progression over and awards **1 Overflow Point**. Your Galvanized skills, Galvanizers, and completed challenges are retained.
- Added clearer guidance when Overflow is ready, a dedicated Challenges tab, and updated navigation icons.

## Skill Presets and Controls

- Presets now follow your chosen **spending priority**, buying prerequisites first and saving points for the next skill instead of spending them on a cheaper skill farther down the list.
- Added a priority view with drag-to-reorder, move-up/down controls, and automatic scrolling while dragging. Galvanized skills appear separately from skills that cost points.
- Preset priority changes are saved immediately. Galvanization only completes once your progress has been saved successfully.
- Simplified skill details and requirement counts. Assign and Unassign buttons keep a consistent size, with a stacked layout on narrow screens.
- Fixed outside taps on skill popups potentially activating controls underneath on touchscreens.

## Fixes

- Fixed Influence gathering becoming unavailable with a full worker pool at certain Influence balances. The fix also applies to automatic gathering and works with existing saves.
- Fixed some Galvanized skills preventing production from resuming after Overflow.
- Added a Steam recovery option for progress made while Steam integration was unavailable. Recover that progress into your Steam profile while preserving the original saves.
- Fixed **First Strange Matter** failing to unlock when previously earned Strange Matter had already been spent.
- Fixed a failed update-interval change discarding a newer selection, including **Default**.
