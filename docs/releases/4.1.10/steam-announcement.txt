I missed posting an announcement with the last update, so this one is getting proper patch notes.

[h2]Transcendence and Discovery[/h2]
Overflow is now Transcendence. Spend your first Transcendence Point in Avocato to unlock Discovery and replace Science and Research.

Discovery grows facility production. Unlock Elevation for Cash and Bots, then Enlightenment for panel lifetime. Each later bar sends time into the bar before it, setting off a chain of completions. Avocato also has permanent speed and power upgrades for the new system.

[h2]New challenges and augments[/h2]
[list]
[*]Seven Quantum challenges, each awarding 2 Catalysts: No Science, Short Circuit, Grounded, Built by Hand, Hands Off, Commitment Issues and Supply Shortage.
[*]Reality Skill Points and selected Quantum upgrades are inactive during these challenges. Your Reality progress is preserved.
[*]Four Manual Labour augments and ten Swarm and Fragment augments.
[*]Galvanizers are now Catalysts, and Galvanized skills are now Fractured skills. You can Fracture during challenges and preview the effect before spending a Catalyst.
[/list]

[h2]Skills and progression[/h2]
[list]
[*]Stellar Memory banks newly generated SRS charge on Infinity and Quantum resets to strengthen SRS augments.
[*]Stellar Sacrifices generates your highest owned facility, including megastructures.
[*]Hot Start is non-refundable. Banking, Investment and Stellar Memory are refundable. A first-time warning explains non-refundable assignments.
[*]Facility price growth and megastructure costs and production have been rebalanced.
[*]After your first Quantum, new runs start with an Assembly Line.
[*]The sixth Avotation secret completes automatically when reached with Discovery unlocked.
[/list]

[h2]Speedruns and fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Personal bests survive Reset Save, with current-run comparisons, assistance indicators and a First Transcendence milestone.
[*]New records count time played plus Stored Time spent. Time sitting unused in your Stored Time bank does not count.
[*]Fixed Permanent 2× Bots losing its effect after Reset Save.
[*]Fixed cleared presets and retired research returning, rapid Tinker input, and several challenge and augment issues.
[*]Improved production details, skill previews, scrolling and dialogs.
[/list]

Thanks to everyone reporting bugs and testing the beta. You've helped catch a lot of the awkward interactions in this update.
