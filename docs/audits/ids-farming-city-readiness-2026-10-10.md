## Work and pacing

Three founders plus two residents per supported Home give at most 15 workers. Active jobs divide labor by focus weights, renormalized over only funded active jobs; one construction or shipment runs at once alongside production. Upkeep is Balanced 15%, Supplies 4%, Growth 20%, Travel 2%. Weights (Food, Materials, Tools, Goods, Build, Ship): Balanced 1/1/.8/.8/1.6/1.3; Supplies 1.5/1.5/1.2/1.2/1/.8; Growth .9/.9/.9/.6/3/.8; Travel .8/.8/.7/.7/1/3. Travel uses Balanced before Hall. Work advances by workers × (1−upkeep) × weight/sum(active weights) × fracture speed × seconds. Retained camp export is a separate owner, capped at 2.7 Food/Materials per minute before fractures; old camp stocks are not duplicated.

Fracture speed is 1 + 0.05 × permanently owned node count, including during an active challenge. Temporary Built by Hand Manual Labour and unspent Catalysts add nothing. Farming work and camp export gain speed; Weathering uses elapsed simulation time, not the fracture multiplier. Twelve nodes mean ×1.6 work/export, not a guarantee that the entire village completes in exactly 1/1.6 of the time.
