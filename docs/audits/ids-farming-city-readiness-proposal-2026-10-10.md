# Farming final stretch — approved and implemented, 10 October 2026

This proposal is superseded by the [implementation audit](ids-farming-city-readiness-2026-10-10.md). Matthew approved Intensive Cultivation, Guild Workshop and Town Market, and moving the sixth Farming Catalyst to the extended readiness endpoint. The first five Farming rewards remain unchanged; the projects do not award extra Catalysts.

The implementation audit records the exact prerequisites, funded costs, worker-seconds, production benefits, focus allocation, source-derived pacing and browser evidence. First Cities remains the future endpoint with “More coming soon.”

Matthew explicitly rejected backward compatibility for completed Farming prototypes because this version has never deployed. There is no grandfathered completed village, reopened terminal economy, additional Farming migration/version or repayment path. Current completed state requires all three projects, six supported Homes and six connections. This decision does not remove released public-save compatibility or separately approved migration safeguards.

Already funded jobs within the current run retain their paid inputs, work and output across an upgrade. That receipt ownership is ordinary concurrent gameplay behavior, not compatibility for old completed prototypes.

The seventh Forager point placement and the Farming award-banking policy remain unanswered. This implementation chooses neither. Production publication and real-save access remain held pending the separate storage-isolation decision.
